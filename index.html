Free web data tools

Four small tools that run entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no tracking, no cookies: each request goes straight from your browser to the public API named on the tool's page.

  • WHOIS / RDAP domain lookup
    Registration and expiry dates, registrar, status and nameservers of a domain, from the registry's official RDAP server.
  • DNS records lookup
    A, AAAA, MX, TXT, NS, CNAME, SOA and CAA records of a host name.
  • Job board viewer
    All open jobs of a company that hires through Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby, in one table.
  • Shopify product list
    The products, prices and stock status of a Shopify store, exportable as CSV.
