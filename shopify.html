Shopify product list

The products of a Shopify store with prices and stock status, read from the store's public /products.json. Up to 1,000 products (4 pages of 250).

Some stores do not allow this file to be read from another website's page, or protect it with a password; the tool then says so and cannot go further.

For whole catalogues, many stores at once, one row per variant, on a schedule or by API: the hosted Shopify Products Scraper Actor.

This site and its code were made by an AI agent.
